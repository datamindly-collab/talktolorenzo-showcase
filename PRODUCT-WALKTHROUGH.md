# Product walkthrough

TalkToLorenzo has three kinds of users: **members** who work with the AI team on the dashboard, **visitors** who talk to a member's personal AI sales link, and **campaign participants** such as those in the 60-Day Accelerator.

## Part 1: The member's workspace

### 1. Start with a real objective

The member begins with a business question, project, or goal rather than choosing a generic chatbot prompt. A clearly visible "+ New project" button turns a goal into a project with a duration and phases.

### 2. Work with the right specialist

The product routes the interaction to a relevant specialist mode, for example strategy, growth, communication, social media, or knowledge organisation. While a project is active, every specialist conversation is attached to it automatically.

### 3. Preserve useful context

TalkToLorenzo keeps track of the active work so the member can return without reconstructing the entire situation. Relevant context and the member's own knowledge are brought forward while the interface stays focused on the current task.

### 4. Turn the conversation into follow-through

Outputs become saved projects, journeys, tasks, resources, decisions, and next steps. A short daily check-in ("How did today's step go?") keeps the project moving, and Lorenzo answers with the next step.

### 5. Teach the team

The member builds a knowledge pack about their business: documents, a product catalogue, photos, and videos. The whole AI team, and the member's sales link, answer from it.

### 6. Duplicate what works

- **Share a knowledge pack:** a team leader sends a join link to the team chat. Members who join can use the pack in their own conversations and on their own sales links, and they always see the leader's latest version.
- **Share a best practice:** a campaign that worked becomes a shareable template. The recipient adopts it in one click and gets their own project, rewritten for their business and in their own language. The sender sees progress, never conversations.

### 7. Continue or hand off

The next session resumes from the previous stopping point. A qualified interaction can be routed to a human with the collected context and attribution information.

## Part 2: The visitor on a personal sales link

A visitor opens `talktolorenzo.com/u/<name>`, for example from a social post or a WhatsApp message.

1. **Greeting.** The member's AI assistant introduces itself as an AI and offers a two-minute conversation, in the visitor's browser language.
2. **Why are you here?** One short, empathetic sentence, then a question with a few buttons, for example "I work alone / 2–5 people / I have a team, but everything depends on me". The visitor can always answer in their own words.
3. **What matters most right now?** The challenges for the chosen path, as buttons.
4. **Mirror and advice.** "So right now X is what matters to you, which makes sense because…", followed by one concrete piece of advice and how the member's offer helps. This is the first time a brand or product name appears.
5. **Offer.** The member's own offer and link: a free trial, a store, a viewing, or a call.
6. **Open questions.** Product, price, and "show me everything" questions are answered from the member's knowledge, with product photos and videos as cards.

At every step, a "💬 Talk to {member}" button opens a contact card. The member receives the lead together with the path and challenge the visitor chose. Read-aloud and voice dictation are built in, and the input stays on screen on mobile Safari.

### Example templates

| Template | First question | Example paths |
|---|---|---|
| General | "What brought you here?" | A solution for myself · Helping someone else · Just looking |
| Entrepreneurs and coaches | "How big is your team?" | Solo · 2–5 people · A team, but it all depends on me |
| Network-marketing distributor | "What interests you most?" | Products for me · Products for a family member · The business opportunity |
| Real estate | "What are you planning?" | Buy · Rent · Invest · Sell |

Wellness and business-opportunity paths follow strict rules: wellness goals rather than conditions, no health or income promises, "results vary", and no health data is requested.

## Part 3: The 60-Day Accelerator participant

1. **Landing page.** Five AI specialists working on one goal for 60 days, in the visitor's language.
2. **Plan with the assistant.** The visitor describes the business, a concrete 60-day goal, the budget, and what is missing. The assistant writes a one-page plan with three phases and a daily rhythm, and can email it.
3. **Apply in the conversation.** An editable summary card plus consent, or a form as a fallback.
4. **Human decision.** The founder replies within 48 hours. Accepted applicants receive an email with a link to the dashboard; others receive a kind reply and an invitation to the next round.
5. **Start on the dashboard.** The participant chooses a start date and pays in place, without leaving the page.
6. **Project ready.** The 60-day project is created from the plan, and Lorenzo starts with the first step of phase one.
7. **Daily rhythm.** One small step a day and a two-minute check-in. A personal conversation with the founder is one tap away. Milestone emails arrive on day 30, 55, 60, and +7; continuing is optional and without pressure.

## Example applications

### Founder support

- Clarify a business problem
- Build and refine an action plan as a project
- Save tasks and resources
- Return for the next decision or checkpoint

### Team leaders and network marketing

- Share one up-to-date knowledge pack with the whole team
- Give every team member a sales link that answers from it
- Pass on successful campaigns as best practices

### Real estate

- Understand buyer, renter, investor, or seller intent
- Gather structured qualification information
- Provide an appropriate guided journey
- Route the opportunity to the relevant person

### E-commerce

- Understand a customer's underlying need
- Guide product discovery with product cards
- Preserve context across the decision journey
- Connect assistance to conversion workflows

## Languages

The product works end to end in English, Spanish, and Hungarian: dashboard, sales links, campaign pages, emails, and shared best practices. The visitor's own language decides, buttons follow the language of each reply, and knowledge written in one language can answer questions asked in another. Multilingual delivery is handled as a product and interaction-design concern, not merely a translation afterthought.
